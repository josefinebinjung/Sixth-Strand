There are three ways out. None of them are good.

Start alone

Eighteen to thirty months without income, no positioning on day one, and no distribution. Most people underestimate this by a year.

Buy a business

Seven figures of equity and debt for a company you did not design. Acquisition has become harder, not easier, and the good targets have several buyers.

Take another job

The fastest option, and the reason you are reading this page.

We help you leverage the one asset you already own and no one can fake: experience.

We build the company. You keep the name on the door.

01

Position

We define what the business sells, to whom, at what price, and why you specifically are the person who should sell it. Your domain, your credibility, a commercial infrastructure shaped around it.

02

Build

Entity, contracts, brand, site, pricing, delivery model, CRM, and the acquisition system. 8 weeks. You get an operating company, not a strategy document.

03

Grow

We run outbound, partnerships, and pipeline for and alongside you until the business is producing revenue on its own motion. You handle the clients we’ve onboarded. You are on sales calls from week 1 and stay part of our community to learn, share and grow. We teach you sales, process, retention, and most importantly, AI. That is a condition of the engagement, not a suggestion.

04

Hand over

Accounts, playbooks, relationships, tech, clients, and all IP transfer to you. Sixth Strand steps back to a minority position and stays available to support retention and scale.

This is the wrong firm for most people.

  • Anyone looking for passive income. This business is built to leverage YOU.
  • Anyone unwilling to be on sales calls in the first month, mainly because you need to learn the process, your clients and AI which we believe lay the foundation of scaling your business.
  • Anyone who wants a business in a field they have no standing in. We build on your existing authority. Without it, the timeline doubles and we would both regret it.
  • Anyone who wants to buy their way to certainty. We will show you the method, the terms, provide you the infrastructure, and hold your hand. In fact, we will do most of the foundation and execution, but we will not take on clients who don’t have the drive to become best in their field and stay with us long term.
